1Introduction

Tarot Glow ("the app", "we", "our") is a tarot card reading app for Android, published by Sagar (the "Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Tarot Glow is designed to work fully offline and does not require you to create an account or sign in to use any feature.

By downloading or using Tarot Glow, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2Information we collect

Tarot Glow does not require registration, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information, and does not include social login, chat, or any user-generated content features.

The only information collected about you comes from the third-party advertising service the app uses to display ads (see Third-party services below) — we do not operate our own servers that collect or store your personal data.

3Information stored only on your device

To make the app work, Tarot Glow saves a small amount of data locally on your device (never transmitted to us or any server we control):

  • Your selected language and appearance (light/dark/system theme)
  • Your daily tarot card and reading history, so today's card stays the same throughout the day
  • Your daily reminder notification preference and chosen reminder time

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data/storage from your device's app settings, permanently deletes it.

4How information is used

The locally-stored information described above is used only to run the app's own features — showing your daily card, remembering your language and theme, and sending your optional daily reminder. Information collected by our advertising partner is used to display and measure ads, as described below.

5Third-party services (advertising)

Tarot Glow is free to use and is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show ads (and, where applicable, personalize them), Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate (coarse) location, derived from IP address
  • App interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks, session length)

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policy, not by us directly. You can learn more about how Google uses this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
Tarot Glow itself does not integrate any analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs beyond Google AdMob. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6Your advertising choices

You can limit ad personalization or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time:

  • On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads and turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization," or reset your advertising ID.
  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage personalization across Google services.

Opting out reduces ad personalization but you will still see ads, since they fund the app's free availability.

7App permissions

Tarot Glow requests the following Android permissions, used strictly for the purposes below:

PermissionPurpose
InternetLoading ads, and checking connectivity so the app can let you know when a full experience needs a connection.
Access Network StateDetecting whether your device is online or offline, so the app can adapt (readings still work fully offline).
Post NotificationsSending your optional daily tarot card reminder, if you turn it on in Settings.
Receive Boot CompletedRe-scheduling your daily reminder after your device restarts, if notifications are enabled.

8Data security

Because Tarot Glow doesn't operate its own backend or user accounts, there is no central database of your personal information for us to protect or that could be breached on our end. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own operating system security (screen lock, encryption, etc.). Data handled by Google AdMob is secured according to Google's own security practices.

9Children's privacy

Tarot Glow is intended for general, entertainment audiences and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age defined by applicable local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Tarot content is presented for entertainment and personal reflection only (see Entertainment disclaimer).

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so we can address it — though as noted above, the app does not collect personal information directly in the first place.

10Data retention & your choices

Since all app data lives on your own device, you are always in control of it:

  • Change your language, theme, or reminder settings any time from within the app's Settings screen.
  • Clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Tarot Glow → Storage → Clear data) to remove locally saved data immediately.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

For anything related to advertising data collected by Google, use the ad choices described above or Google's own account/privacy controls.

11International users

Tarot Glow is available to users worldwide. If you access the app from outside your country of residence, be aware that information processed by third-party services like Google AdMob may be transferred to and processed in other countries, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for users in the European Economic Area).
